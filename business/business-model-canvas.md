# Business Model Canvas
## Customer Segments
* **Primary Customer Segment**: Developers working on multilingual app development projects
	+ Average team size: 5-10 developers
	+ Average project duration: 6-12 months
	+ Average project budget: $100,000 - $500,000 (THB 3,500,000 - 17,500,000)
* **Secondary Customer Segment**: Development teams and agencies working on multilingual app development projects
	+ Average team size: 10-50 developers
	+ Average project duration: 6-24 months
	+ Average project budget: $500,000 - $2,000,000 (THB 17,500,000 - 70,000,000)
* **Tertiary Customer Segment**: Enterprise companies with large-scale multilingual app development projects
	+ Average team size: 50-100 developers
	+ Average project duration: 6-36 months
	+ Average project budget: $2,000,000 - $10,000,000 (THB 70,000,000 - 350,000,000)

## Value Propositions
* **Automated JSON Translation**: Reduce manual translation errors and increase translation speed by up to 90%
* **AI-powered Translation**: Improve translation accuracy and consistency across languages and dialects
* **Streamlined Workflow**: Simplify the translation process and reduce project timelines by up to 50%
* **Cost Savings**: Reduce translation costs by up to 70% compared to manual translation methods
* **Scalability**: Handle large-scale multilingual app development projects with ease

## Channels
* **Digital Marketing**: Online advertising, social media, and content marketing to reach developers and development teams
* **Partnerships**: Collaborate with development agencies, software companies, and industry associations to expand reach
* **Word-of-Mouth**: Leverage customer referrals and case studies to build credibility and attract new customers
* **Trade Shows and Events**: Attend industry conferences and trade shows to showcase the product and build relationships
* **Content Marketing**: Create informative blog posts, videos, and webinars to educate developers about the benefits of automated JSON translation

## Customer Relationships
* **Support and Training**: Provide comprehensive support and training to customers to ensure successful adoption and usage
* **Community Building**: Foster a community of developers and users to share knowledge, best practices, and feedback
* **Regular Updates and Feedback**: Regularly update the product and solicit feedback from customers to improve the overall experience
* **Account Management**: Assign dedicated account managers to large enterprise customers to ensure personalized support and service
* **Customer Success**: Focus on customer success and satisfaction to drive retention and word-of-mouth marketing

## Revenue Streams
* **Subscription-based Model**: Offer a monthly or annual subscription to access the automated JSON translation tool
	+ Monthly subscription: $500 - $2,000 (THB 17,500 - 70,000)
	+ Annual subscription: $5,000 - $20,000 (THB 175,000 - 700,000)
* **Per-Project Pricing**: Offer a one-time fee for each project completed using the automated JSON translation tool
	+ Small projects: $1,000 - $5,000 (THB 35,000 - 175,000)
	+ Medium projects: $5,000 - $20,000 (THB 175,000 - 700,000)
	+ Large projects: $20,000 - $100,000 (THB 700,000 - 3,500,000)
