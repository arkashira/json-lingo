# Customer Journey Map
## Overview
The customer journey map for json-lingo outlines the phases a potential customer goes through from initial awareness to expansion of the product. The map highlights trigger events, friction points, user emotions, opportunities to delight, and key metrics for each phase.

## Aware
* **Trigger Event**: Encounter with manual JSON translation issues in multilingual app development, leading to errors and inefficiencies.
* **Friction Points**: Lack of awareness about automated JSON translation tools, perceived complexity of integrating new tools into existing development workflows.
* **User Emotions**: Frustration, curiosity.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Introduction to json-lingo through targeted developer community outreach, showcasing success stories and ease of integration.
* **Metric**: Website traffic, social media engagement (e.g., Twitter, LinkedIn), relevant keyword searches (e.g., "automated JSON translation").

## Consider
* **Trigger Event**: Recognition of the need for an automated solution due to increasing project complexity or expanding into new languages.
* **Friction Points**: Concerns about tool compatibility, learning curve, and potential costs.
* **User Emotions**: Interest, skepticism.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Offering free trials, demos, or a limited free version to test json-lingo's capabilities, showcasing customer testimonials and case studies.
* **Metric**: Free trial sign-ups, demo requests, email newsletter subscriptions.

## Try
* **Trigger Event**: Decision to test json-lingo based on positive reviews, recommendations, or successful trial experiences.
* **Friction Points**: Initial setup challenges, integration issues with existing tools or workflows.
* **User Emotions**: Hope, impatience.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Prompt and helpful support (e.g., documentation, community forums, direct support), ensuring seamless onboarding and integration.
* **Metric**: Activation rate (from sign-up to first successful use), support ticket resolution rate, user retention rate.

## Adopt
* **Trigger Event**: Successful integration and positive experience with json-lingo, leading to regular use.
* **Friction Points**: Potential for occasional errors or compatibility issues, need for ongoing support and updates.
* **User Emotions**: Satisfaction, loyalty.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Regular updates with new features, proactive support, and community engagement to foster a sense of belonging among users.
* **Metric**: Paid conversion rate, customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores, net promoter score (NPS).

## Expand
* **Trigger Event**: Recognition of json-lingo's value, leading to increased usage or recommendation to others.
* **Friction Points**: Potential limitations in the current plan, need for additional features or support for more languages.
* **User Emotions**: Advocacy, anticipation for future developments.
* **Opportunities to Delight**: Offering premium services, priority support, or early access to new features for loyal customers, encouraging user-generated content and community participation.
* **Metric**: Revenue growth, customer retention rate, referral rate, community engagement metrics (e.g., forum participation, user-generated content).
